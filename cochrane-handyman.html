What Cochrane homeowners usually call me for

Move-in punch lists

Curtain rods, blinds, shelves, TV mounts, door hardware: the list that builds up after you get the keys. I bring the anchors and the stud finder and do it in one visit where possible. See move-in repairs.

Weather-related fixes

Chinook swings from freezing to mild and back are hard on caulking, weatherstripping and doors. A door that sticks in January is usually a hinge or a strike plate, not a new door. More in why doors stick in winter.

Fall and spring prep

Shutting off and draining outdoor taps, re-caulking tubs and windows, small deck and fence repairs before the season. See how to winterize outdoor taps.

Fixtures on existing connections

Faucets, toilets, light fixtures, switches and outlets replaced on the wiring and plumbing you already have. New circuits and permit work go to a licensed electrician or plumber, and I will tell you when that is the case.
